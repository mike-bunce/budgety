Privacy Policy

Bunce Budget · Last updated: October 16, 2026

Bunce Budget (“the app”, “we”) is a private budgeting web app for personal and family finances, developed and operated by Mike Bunce (“the developer”). The app is available at https://mike-bunce.github.io/budgety/. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how you can have it removed.

In short

  • You sign in with your Google Account. The app uses only your verified email address from that sign-in, to decide whether you are allowed in.
  • Budget data lives in a Google Sheet owned by the budget’s owner. It is not copied to any database run by the developer.
  • Budget data is visible only to people on the allow list, which the owner manages in the app’s Admin panel.
  • We do not sell data, show ads, or use data to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • The app does not access your Gmail, Google Drive files, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google service data in your own account.

1. Who is responsible

Each budget has an owner: the person whose Google Account owns the Google Sheet and the Google Apps Script project that powers the app. The owner decides who may use the budget and is responsible for the data in it. The developer, Mike Bunce, is the owner of the budget this app instance serves. Contact details are in section 12.

2. Information the app accesses

Google Account information (sign-in)

When you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google gives the app a signed ID token. The app requests only the basic sign-in permissions (OpenID, email and profile). The token can contain your name, profile picture, Google account identifier and email address. The app checks the token with Google and uses only your email address; it does not keep your name, picture or account identifier.

The app does not request access to any other Google data and cannot read or change files, email, calendar or contacts in your Google Account.

Budget data in the owner’s Google Sheet

After you are verified as an allowed user, the app reads from and writes to the budget spreadsheet on your behalf. This data includes:

  • Bank and credit-card transactions: dates, descriptions, amounts, balances, cardholder names, categories and verification marks.
  • Account starting balances and calculated balances.
  • Budget categories, groups, monthly budget amounts, income targets and category-to-category adjustments.
  • Projections and monthly totals calculated from the above.

When you import a CSV or TSV statement, the file is read in your browser and the transaction rows are sent to the app’s server code, which adds new rows to the spreadsheet. The uploaded file itself is not kept.

Allow list

The email addresses of allowed users and administrators are stored by the owner in the Apps Script project’s settings (Script Properties).

Data stored in your browser

  • Session storage: your Google sign-in token, so a page refresh does not force a new sign-in. It is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Local storage: your theme choice and the forecast numbers you type on the Graphs page. These stay on your device and are not sent to the server.

The app itself sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Google’s sign-in service may set its own cookies, governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Who can see the data

  • Allowed users can view all data in the budget and can add, edit and import transactions and categories. There is no per-user partitioning of budget data.
  • Administrators can do everything allowed users can, and can also add or remove people on the allow list.
  • The owner controls the underlying Google Sheet and Apps Script project through their own Google Account, and can see everything in them.
  • Anyone not on the allow list is refused access, even if they have a valid Google Account.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not share it with third parties for advertising, data brokering or any other purpose. Information is disclosed only to the allowed users described above, to the service providers that technically run the app (section 5), and where required by law.

5. Service providers

  • Google provides sign-in (Google Identity Services), the server code (Google Apps Script), data storage (Google Sheets) and short-term caching. Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy and terms.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) serves the app’s web pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP address and browser type when pages are loaded, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Budget data is never sent to GitHub.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Bunce Budget’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide and improve the app’s user-facing features described in this policy.
  • Google user data is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale of assets with notice to users.
  • Google user data is not used for serving ads, including personalized, retargeted or interest-based ads.
  • Humans do not read Google user data, except with your consent, for security purposes, to comply with law, or as needed to operate the app. The developer, as owner, can see the budget spreadsheet because it is stored in their own Google Account.
  • Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

7. How information is protected

  • All communication between your browser, the app’s server code and Google uses HTTPS encryption in transit. Google encrypts Google Sheets data at rest.
  • Every request to the server must include a Google sign-in token. The server verifies the token with Google, checks it was issued for this app, and then checks the verified email against the allow list before it reads or changes any data. Admin actions require administrator status.
  • The server only accepts a fixed list of app actions and rejects anything else.
  • The Google Sheet is not publicly shared. Access to the spreadsheet itself is limited to the owner’s Google Account permissions, and the app’s server code acts on the owner’s behalf only for people on the allow list.
  • To avoid repeated verification calls, the server briefly caches a one-way hash of your sign-in token alongside your verified email for up to 10 minutes.

No online service can be guaranteed completely secure. If you believe your access or data has been compromised, please contact us promptly.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Budget data stays in the owner’s Google Sheet until the owner edits or deletes it. The owner can delete any or all data at any time directly in Google Sheets.
  • Allow-list emails are kept until the owner removes them in the Admin panel. Removing a person immediately ends their access to the app; it does not delete budget data they previously entered, which belongs to the budget.
  • Sign-in token cache on the server expires automatically within 10 minutes.
  • Browser data can be removed by closing the tab (session storage) or clearing the site’s data in your browser (local storage).
  • Server logs: Google Apps Script may keep standard execution logs visible only to the owner, under Google’s own retention policies. The app does not intentionally log your personal information.

To request that your email be removed from the allow list, or that data relating to you be deleted from the budget, contact us using section 12. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Your choices and access controls

  • You can stop using the app and close the tab at any time.
  • You can review and revoke the app’s access to your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can ask the owner to show, correct or delete your information.

10. Children

Bunce Budget is intended for adults managing household finances and is not directed to children under 13. Anyone who is not on the allow list cannot access any data.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles Google user data or other information, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date. The policy is linked from every page of the app. If a change materially affects how Google user data is used, we will also tell allowed users in the app.
